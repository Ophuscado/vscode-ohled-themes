# Changelog

## [1.33.0] - 2026-10-05

- Aqua and Fuchsia no longer duplicate Cyan and Magenta, and six nearly identical colours are told apart: BlanchedAlmond, DarkOrchid, LawnGreen, Navy, SeaShell and Wheat. No theme is renamed.
- Comments, keywords, inactive labels, line numbers and the dim terminal colours keep a readable contrast in every theme.
- Notebook status icons take the theme colour instead of red.
- Hovered, focused and selected items keep their text readable; the terminal's black is black.
- Colour keys brought up to date with current VS Code.
- New icon, shorter README and a smaller package.
